Connect to your PC

Enter your computer's Phantom backend address (e.g. http://192.168.1.50:8000). Your PC must be on and Phantom running.

💡 Auto mode: uses your PC when reachable, portable cloud when not. The 🔄 button cycles auto → PC → portable.

🚀 Getting started (iPhone)
  1. PC: keep Phantom running — bash scripts/run.sh or the tray app.
  2. Same Wi-Fi: enter http://<PC-IP>:8000 above (PC on the same network) and Connect.
  3. Anywhere (off campus/home): on the PC run bash scripts/tunnel.sh, open the https://…trycloudflare.com URL here, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
  4. Portable cloud: deploy the Worker (bash cloud/deploy.sh) and paste its URL — works even with your PC off.

🔐 If you set an access token on the PC, enter it in the token field.
